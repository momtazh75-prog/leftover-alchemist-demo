    What changed in version 2

    Ingredient suggestions now distinguish cooked and dry rice. You can combine equipment constraints, exclude ingredients, review a food photo, read English receipt text, track pantry dates, export calendar reminders, rate recipes, and save feedback.

    A free prototype, with honest limits

    Recipe suggestions use 14 built-in templates, not live generative AI. Ingredient-fit scores measure required-ingredient coverage, not taste, nutrition, or safety. Food photos need manual ingredient review. Receipt text recognition runs locally and may make mistakes; confirm items and dates before saving.

    Your data stays in this browser

    Photos, receipts, pantry items, meal logs, and feedback are not sent to the project team or stored in a shared database. Browser storage can be cleared, especially on university computers. Download important records before leaving. The public link shows the same app on another device, but not your private local data.

    Reminders and dates

    In-app reminders work while the site is open. Calendar export lets you import reminders into a calendar app. Guidance estimates assume appropriate storage and do not establish an actual expiry date. Generic items get a check-in reminder rather than an invented shelf-life prediction.
