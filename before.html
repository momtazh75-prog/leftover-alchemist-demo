The idea in action

what’s already there.

A simple prototype for the Alchemists team’s MGMT 461 innovation journey.

01

Take a look in your kitchen

Enter ingredients you have. Choose your time, portions, cookware, and dietary preferences. Use up suitable ingredients you already own.

02

Find a meal that fits

The free offline recipe engine matches your ingredients to 14 built-in recipes. Open a match to see quantities and steps. Recipes needing an extra ingredient are clearly marked.

03

Cook, then capture the result

Log a completed meal with a rating, participant ID, feedback, and optional photo. Export the CSV for your milestone evidence. Keep original photos separately.

04

Learn and improve

Look at feedback and failed attempts during your pilot. Make a change, test again, and compare outcomes. Track discarded food separately over comparable baseline and pilot periods.

About this demo. It works without accounts, internet, paid services, or API keys. It uses built-in recipe rules rather than live generative AI. Photo uploads attach evidence to a meal; they do not scan your fridge. Browser records are not shared or backed up, so export important work.

Use ingredients that are safe to eat and follow package storage instructions. This demo cannot assess freshness or guarantee allergy safety. Review every ingredient and product label before cooking.
